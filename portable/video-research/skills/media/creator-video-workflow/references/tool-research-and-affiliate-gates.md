# Research standard for adopting video tooling

This workflow serves the user's short/long video monetization and Shopee product-affiliate work. Prefer demonstrated editorial usefulness over the number of tools installed.

Before adopting a video skill/MCP:
1. Retrieve official source, exact revision, license, maintenance activity, platform/runtime support, required permissions, credentials, paid APIs and upload destinations.
2. Search actual Claude community discussions, relevant public GitHub issues/discussions, and independent forums. Record direct sources and dates, including failures and criticism. Closed Discord/paid communities are not inspected merely by seeing a search snippet; report access limits.
3. Distinguish GitHub stars/forks/downloads/install events from active people. None proves published output quality, audience retention or profit.
4. Find actual produced videos or editable projects. A tutorial's own view count does not establish the performance of a video made with that workflow. A video author selling a course or service can provide a demonstration but has an incentive to promote; label performance/income statements as self-reports unless independently substantiated.
5. Track an evidence ladder separately: renders successfully; passes visual/audio/product-fidelity QA; has public audience results; has attributable validated revenue net of the relevant costs. Passing a lower rung never proves a higher rung.
6. Inspect a representative actual render before calling the tool operationally validated. Evaluate full playback where possible, selected frame checks plus audio/metadata, and disclose any parts not reviewed. A contact sheet cannot prove timing or audio quality.
7. Compare the platform's native asset library, reusable characters, built-in agent/tools and the host agent's current browser integration before proposing another extension or MCP. Read the full current interfaces, including optional reference parameters, before declaring a capability absent. Add only the missing capability; keep one renderer/compositor owner per video, one editorial owner and one controller per active browser tab. Start with an isolated project, not a broad always-on MCP.
   For asset-driven automation, trace the complete path through reviewed source: local upload or native selection -> project-scoped remote asset -> actual reference binding -> submit -> persisted job/output -> recovery. A README feature, upload endpoint or reference parameter alone does not prove the production path uses it. Distinguish open bridge code from a separate closed-source engine, and do not transfer a license or audit conclusion between them.
8. If evidence is insufficient, retain candidate status and say what proof is missing. Do not invent claims of widespread adoption, virality, conversions or earnings to complete a shortlist.

Shopee affiliate lane:
- Confirm country/market, program and surface (Shopee Video versus an external social platform) before applying policy. Don't transfer Singapore/Malaysia/Philippines rules to Indonesia.
- Use the real product or authorized truthful footage and listing data. Generated visuals are not proof that a product performs that way; no fake testimonials, personal use claims, before/after results, discounts or features.
- Verify current official disclosure, originality, product-link and commission rules. Labelled AI content is not automatically eligible for every program. Don't claim AI means no commission in a market without official scoped evidence.
- Evaluate product-clicks, attributable orders, validated commissions, cancellations/returns and production spend/time from actual analytics, not views alone. Separate estimates and hypotheses from realized results.
- Prefer an editorial test plan using the creator's real product and brand assets before broad adoption; do not batch-publish template variants or spend money without authorization.

Anti-slop review questions (editorial acceptance criteria, not a formula guaranteeing virality):
- Does the video give the target viewer a useful, original payoff rather than merely animate filler text?
- Are product appearance, use, claims, branding and real-world physics truthful and consistent?
- Are composition, type, pacing, transitions, sound and narration intentional and coherent rather than a random effect stack?
- Are subtitles readable and synchronized on the actual target surface?
- Does it avoid repetitive asset recycling, stolen footage, synthetic fake evidence and deceptive impersonation?
- Can we trace every factual claim and commercial-use asset permission?
- Did we inspect the actual export, and is a responsible human approval required before publication?

Check current Remotion runtime licensing separately at https://www.remotion.dev/license. Community wrapper skills may have different terms, dependencies, support and privacy behavior.
