# Evidence-led short and long video workflow

## Research decision and adoption boundary

A completed audit of coding/rendering skills is not a completed survey of generative-video services. Separate generative models (such as Seedance and Veo), production platforms (such as Higgsfield), and composition/editing tools before selecting a pipeline; they can complement one another rather than substitute for one another. Adopt this editorial/affiliate coordinator and the existing native-tool baseline; do not present any third-party production bundle as validated viral/profitable tooling. Read references/tool-selection.md for the selection rationale and references/platform-release-gates.md before making a platform-specific release recommendation. The local FFmpeg baseline has passed synthetic vertical/horizontal encode, probe and full-decode smoke tests; those fixtures prove a technical export path only, not creative quality, retention, sales or revenue. This coordinator does not claim a third-party renderer has been operationally validated.

Research active Claude communities, GitHub, and relevant forums before adding or changing a video-production skill. Check actual published outputs, reproducibility, maintainer/user activity, technical fit, commercial licensing and outcome evidence. GitHub stars, tutorial views and income testimonials do not establish attribution. Separate observed views, self-reported analytics and verifiable commission/payment evidence. If evidence is insufficient, the completed selection decision is DEFER, not an invented success. Keep production tooling unadopted until its permission/license review and a representative sample render/quality check. Viral results and validated revenue are separate evidence requirements for business claims, not prerequisites for a bounded technical pilot. Before any paid generation, confirm the route/account, exact model, authorized references, output count and current quote; include auxiliary image generation, previews, extensions and upscaling in the approved budget. Persist the intent before submitting and record the returned job ID when available. Treat a timeout or missing job ID after dispatch as UNKNOWN, retain its budget reservation, and reconcile existing work before considering a separately authorized retry; a timeout does not prove that no charge occurred.

The user's confirmed affiliate market is Shopee Indonesia, with publishing on Shopee Video, TikTok, YouTube Shorts/long-form, and Instagram Reels. Reuse this context; confirm only if a task concerns a different market or surface. Apply each platform's own policy rather than transferring Singapore/Malaysia/Philippines Shopee rules or confusing external Shopee affiliate content with TikTok Shop's program. Treat product fidelity, authorized footage, truthful demonstrations, disclosure and attributable validated affiliate commissions as release gates; high views alone do not prove affiliate profit. Do not fabricate product capabilities, hands-on experience, before/after effects, reviews, scarcity or discounts.

## Asset-grounded continuity

For this user's multi-scene generative videos, support actual attachment and reuse of approved character, location/background, product/prop and other required assets; repeating descriptions or typing an asset name alone does not satisfy the requirement. Keep a versioned asset registry and per-scene bindings, separating locked appearance/set/product attributes from intentional changes in action, camera and lighting. Treat voice as a separately verified input rather than assuming a character reference includes it.

Verify the selected references and settings by reading back the generation UI or supported API before requesting spending approval. Stop on missing, ambiguous or unsupported required references; never silently drop an asset, substitute a model or reduce the requirement to text-only prompting. Visible attachment proves selection, not that the model followed the reference. Inspect actual clips and scene transitions for identity, wardrobe, background geometry, product fidelity and voice drift; never promise deterministic consistency from reference reuse.

For asset-capable tool research or implementation planning, follow `references/asset-continuity.md`. Keep documented capabilities, source-implemented behavior and live-account verification separate. A plan-only request authorizes research and design artifacts, not plugin installation, login, uploads or generation.

## Production sequence and artifacts

1. Brief: audience problem/interest, value proposition, angle, platform, duration/format, intended action and revenue hypothesis. Compare plausible concepts and explain what evidence favors or weakens each. Inspiration is not demonstrated demand.
2. Research: make a claim ledger with source, date, evidence, confidence and visual requirement. Separate facts, opinion and dramatization; don't fabricate screenshots, earnings, testimonials or experiences. For news and disputed claims seek corroboration.
3. Script: write an accurate hook, promised payoff, structure, transitions and CTA appropriate to the audience. Don't hardcode a universal hook length or guaranteed retention formula. For long-form, use sections and earned progression; for shorts, keep an intelligible, self-contained story rather than blindly cropping the long video.
4. Storyboard/shot list: scene_id, narration/dialogue, on-screen text, visual/shot, source/license, duration estimate, audio, required asset, editor note. Timing is an estimate until a voice read or actual timeline confirms it.
5. Assets: maintain an asset ledger with origin, owner, license and commercial-use restrictions/attribution. Confirm consent for voice/likeness; label synthetic material where platform rules require. Don't treat watermarks, transcript availability, stock labels or AI generation as evidence of unrestricted rights.
6. Produce: use original/licensed assets and the selected actual toolchain; make a draft render, inspect real frames/audio and captions, then correct it. Keep safe text areas and aspect ratio platform-specific. Do not upload or publish merely because local rendering succeeded.
7. QA: check facts, pronunciation, spelling, audio intelligibility/clipping, caption timing, pacing, image/asset quality, mobile readability, black/frozen frames, aspect/resolution/duration and disclosure/ad suitability for the target platform. Inspect an actual exported file and probe metadata; list tests not performed. No claim of full video QA from metadata alone.
8. Package/distribute: propose truthful titles, thumbnail concepts and descriptions with required credits/disclosures. User approval before publishing, scheduling, paid services, ads or account changes unless already explicitly authorized. Read back exact target after any remote write.
9. Learn: import actual analytics with provenance, comparable windows, platform definitions, sample size and organic/paid differences. Track the metric relevant to the hypothesis: impressions/CTR when available, watch time/retention/completion, conversion/revenue and production cost/time. These are diagnostic questions, not universal performance targets. Write what the data supports and what it cannot establish; log the next experiment without claiming causality from one observation.
